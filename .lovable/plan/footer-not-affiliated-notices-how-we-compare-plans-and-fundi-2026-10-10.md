## 3. New page: /how-we-compare ("How we compare plans")
Needed because the footer links to it and no such page exists yet. Plain language, describing only what the site really does:
- Where the data comes from (CMS 2027 Landscape and Plan Benefit Package files, Michigan only; star ratings labeled by year).
- True yearly cost: what's included.
- Worst case: what's included, and that an Advantage plan's out-of-pocket limit excludes drugs.
- How "Best match" is chosen: estimated cost plus your answers (doctor flexibility, peace of mind vs. lower premium, travel, extras). Plans within about $250 rank the lower worst case first. Special Needs Plans are listed last unless you qualify.
- What we never use: insurer name, payments or donations.
- Limits: estimates, not quotes; check the formulary; confirm hospitals are in network.
Added to the sitemap, with its own page title and description.
